# SwampHacks XII rules for humans and AI agents

- **Every agent reads this before building, submitting or writing Devpost text.** If a task would break a rule here, stop and ask the team.
- All code is written during the event (Oct 16, 8:30 PM to Oct 18). Anything built before is disqualified.
- AI tools are allowed but must be disclosed on Devpost. The project must not be a reskin of an existing AI tool.
- **At most ONE specialized track.** Confirmed by organizers (Discord, Oct 16): the 4 specialized tracks are Hardware & Physical Computing, Entertainment & Creative Technology, Best AI/ML, and First Timers. We enter **Best Use of AI/ML**. Challenges (sponsor, MLH, SwampHacks-specific) can be several.
- Devpost due **Sun Oct 18, 8:00 AM ET**. Live demo max 3 minutes (+1 minute questions). Demo video max 2 minutes.

## Official rules (Devpost)
Source: https://swamphacks-xii.devpost.com/rules (read 2026-10-16)

1. Only accepted hackers may participate. Organizers, mentors, volunteers, sponsors and judges may not be on a team.
2. Teams of 2 to 4 hackers. One project per team.
3. The project must be built in the 36-hour window. Work from before the event is disqualified.
4. Submit on Devpost AND present at the Project Expo. Judging happens only at the expo.
5. On Devpost, mark every track and challenge we enter.
6. Every team is in the General Track (Overall) automatically.
7. **Opt in to at most one specialized track.** Enter as many challenges as we meet the requirements for.
8. Do not submit the project to any other hackathon running at the same time.
9. The submission needs a project description, a demo, and an accessible code repository (our public GitHub repo).
10. Public frameworks, libraries, APIs and open-source tools are allowed but must be credited (README "Credits" section).
11. AI/LLM tools may be used but must be disclosed. No simple reskins of existing AI tools.
12. Judging is in person, science-fair style, at the Project Expo.
13. Demographic challenges (for example First Timers) need the stated eligibility.
14. Live demo: 3 minutes max, plus 1 minute for judge questions. Devpost demo video: 2 minutes max.

## General judging criteria (all projects)
1. **Technical understanding:** can we explain how and why we built it, the choices and the challenges?
2. **Innovation and creativity:** how original is the idea and the approach?
3. **Functionality:** does it work reliably in the demo and do what we claim?
4. **Design and usability:** is it intuitive and well designed for its users?
5. **Purpose and audience:** does it deliver real value to the people it targets?
6. **Track and challenge relevance:** is it relevant to what we entered?
7. **Sponsor challenge criteria:** sponsor judges may add their own criteria (announced at the opening ceremony).

## MLH Code of Conduct (summary)
Source: https://github.com/MLH/mlh-policies/blob/main/code-of-conduct.md (updated 2026-04-16)

- Be respectful. Harassment and abuse are never tolerated, at the venue, at socials, and online.
- No offensive comments about gender, age, sexual orientation, disability, appearance, body size, race, religion, class, economic or veteran status.
- No sexualized images or material in hacks or at the event. No intimidation, stalking, doxxing, or harassing photography or recording.
- If asked to stop, stop immediately. Violations can mean removal from the event.
- **Report:** incidents@mlh.io or +1 409 202 6060 (North America). Reports can be anonymous.

## Rules for AI agents working in this repo
1. Never commit code written before the event start, and never import code from another project.
2. Credit every library, model, dataset and API in the README "Credits" section, with its license.
3. Record every dataset's source URL and license in `ml/data/SOURCES.md`. Do not use data whose license forbids it.
4. Demo data uses fake people only. Never put a real person's name, number, bank data or messages in the repo or the demo.
5. No copyrighted images, logos or music without a license. Sponsor logos only as their brand rules allow.
6. Devpost and README text states which AI tools we used and how.
7. Keep demo flows under 3 minutes and the video under 2 minutes.
8. Do not spam or load-test sponsor APIs (Nessie, Auth0, Tiger Data). Use test tenants and test data.
