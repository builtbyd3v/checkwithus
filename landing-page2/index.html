Protection that runs by itself

Three layers of defence on the phone, and fixed rules, not a chatbot, deciding when money moves.

Scam texts never arrive

A model that runs on the phone filters scam messages before they reach the inbox. Their texts are never sent to a cloud AI.

Scam calls ring as “Possible scam”

Known scam numbers are labelled on the call screen, so they know before they answer.

Nothing to learn

Big text, plain words, one button. “You’re safe” or “Paused, your family will call.”

Risky payments pause

Gift cards, crypto, new payees, big amounts, anything unusual, and any payment started during a call are held for family.

Freeze in one tap

If something looks wrong, one tap sweeps the spending balance into the vault, out of a scammer’s reach.

Allowances for the AI agent

Family can grant limited, expiring permission, such as utility bills under $200 until Nov 30. Inside it, no push.

How it works

From a suspicious payment to a family decision, in three steps.

1

Family sets up the circle

Choose who approves and who just watches. The older adult’s phone gets a protected account and nothing else to manage.

2

Scams are stopped quietly

Texts are filtered, calls are labelled, and the payment rules hold anything that looks like a scam, even the ATM-cash-then-gift-card trick.

3

Family decides in one tap

A push shows the amount, the payee and the reason. Approve or deny, and their phone updates live.

Everyone has a clear role

The family circle decides. The person being protected never has to.
